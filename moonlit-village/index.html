How to play

Six villagers are dealt one secret role each. Identify the werewolf before the village is outnumbered.

Roles in this match
☾

×1 Werewolf

At night, select someone to eliminate. You cannot attack yourself or another werewolf.

✦

×1 Seer

Each night, privately inspect one other living player's alignment.

◇

×1 Guardian

Each night, protect one living player, including yourself. A protected target survives the attack.

♧

×3 Villager

You have no night action. Discuss clues, then vote during the day.

Round flow

Night: the werewolf attacks, the seer investigates and the guardian protects. Day: everyone gives a statement, then votes to eliminate a suspect. The three night prompts still appear even if a role has been eliminated.

Public claims and bluffs

Anyone can bluff a role. Clears and accusations are claims until corroborated; use public votes and contradictions.

How to win

The village wins when the werewolf is eliminated. The werewolf wins if living werewolves equal or outnumber living villagers. In this short game, surviving past day 12 also gives the werewolf victory.

Night, discussion and voting each have a two-minute limit. No action means you skip the ability, pass your speech or abstain from voting. Tied votes eliminate nobody.
